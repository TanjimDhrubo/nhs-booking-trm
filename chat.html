Hi, I'm the TRM Health Assistant. Describe your symptoms and I'll help analyse them. When you're ready, you can send a summary to your doctor and book an appointment — so they already understand your condition before you arrive.
Why send this to my doctor?

When you send your analysis, your doctor receives a clear summary of your symptoms, duration, and relevant details before your appointment. This means:

  • Your doctor already understands your situation when you walk in
  • No need to repeat everything from scratch during consultation
  • More time for actual discussion, diagnosis, and treatment

This information is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult your GP for health concerns. If you're experiencing a medical emergency, call 999 immediately.
